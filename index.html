How DuesPay works

01

Create

Treasurer opens a campaign with a title, exact dues amount, and payment deadline.

02

Collect

Members pay from their wallets. The contract enforces the exact amount and deadline automatically.

03

Spend

Treasurer withdraws for approved expenses. Each withdrawal requires a description stored on-chain.

04

Audit

Any wallet can check who paid, what was collected, and every expense — all public on the blockchain.

Why choose DuesPay

⚡

Zero trust required

Smart contract holds the rules. No treasurer can overspend or collect incorrectly.

🔍

Full transparency

Paid status is public. Every expense has an on-chain record with description and timestamp.

🔒

Pause control

Treasurer can pause a campaign to stop new payments while an issue is resolved.

📋

Member roster

Query getMembers(id) to get the full list of addresses that have paid.
